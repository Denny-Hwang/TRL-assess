# Adoption readiness (ARL)

TRL asks how far a technology has been demonstrated. The Adoption Readiness Level asks what stands
between a working technology and its use — market, money, supply chain, permission. ARL "represents
important factors for private sector uptake beyond technology readiness" (`doe-otc-arl-2025`, p. 1).

The tool implements the DOE Office of Technology Commercialization's _Adoption Readiness Assessment_
(Version: April 2025) at [Adoption Readiness](/arl). It never changes a TRL result, and the two are
never combined.

## The 17 dimensions

:::figure arl-dimensions:::

Rate each dimension against the rubric's Low / Medium / High text, shown on every card, or mark it
N/A. Record the reasoning each time: "Assess the technology solution based on each dimension of the
rubric (Low, Medium, or High Risk, or N/A) and record rationale and details" (p. 2).

## Scope comes first

Record the **technology scope**, the **value chain scope**, the **timeline** ("Best practice is to
consider a 3-5 year commercialization window") and the **policy environment** ("Best practice is to
assume the current policy environment and no further changes") — p. 2.

## From ratings to a number

:::figure arl-lookup:::

The Medium- and High-risk counts are read from the look-up table on p. 13: 1–3 **Low Readiness**,
4–6 **Medium Readiness**, 7–9 **High Readiness**. The source calls the number optional, so the
result page leads with the risk profile. The table is used unmodified.

## How ratings are counted

| Rating                    | Counted as               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Low, Medium, High         | as rated                 |
| N/A with a rationale      | not counted              |
| N/A without a rationale   | **High**                 |
| Unsure                    | **High**                 |
| Not assessed              | **High**                 |
| Rated without a rationale | as rated, with a warning |
| No end-of-project target  | the current rating       |

The High-risk defaults are this tool's conservative convention, not a rule of the source.

**ARL Start** comes from the current ratings. **ARL End** comes from the end-of-project targets and
is labelled _Target — planned, not achieved_. A target that lowers a risk without a planned action
raises a warning.

## Limitations

- **The ratings are yours.** DOE does not review or endorse the result.
- **Dimensions overlap:** "some risks may fall into more than one dimension" (p. 2). Record a risk
  once, where it matters most.
- **Evidence is a reference.** Name the document, or an evidence id from the
  [Evidence Assessment](/assess); ARL ratings are not in the evidence package.

The ARL workbook is described in [Excel output](/guide/excel#adoption-readiness-workbook).
