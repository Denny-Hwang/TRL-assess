# Glossary

Terms marked with a source are quoted from it; the rest are this tool's plain-language definitions.

## The assessment

| Term                  | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TRL**               | Technology Readiness Level: a 1–9 scale for how far a technology has been demonstrated, and in what environment. Not quality, value or programme health.                                            |
| **TRA**               | Technology Readiness Assessment: a formal, _independent_ evaluation of a programme's CTEs under an agency process. This tool produces a self-assessment, which is not a TRA.                        |
| **CTE**               | Critical Technology Element: a part of the system whose maturity decides whether the system works — new, or newly used, with real uncertainty that it will perform. TRAs assess CTEs, not products. |
| **Contiguity**        | The rule that a level counts only if every level below it is achieved. Applied in both tiers.                                                                                                       |
| **Evidence coverage** | _Met_ criteria with at least one non-rejected evidence item ÷ _Met_ criteria.                                                                                                                       |

## Environments and articles

| Term                                  | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Relevant environment**              | "Testing environment that simulates both the most important and most stressing aspects of the operational environment" (`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3, p. 11). Applied to a component at TRL 5, a system/subsystem at 6. |
| **Operational environment**           | "Environment that addresses user operational requirements and specifications required of the final system to include platform/packaging" (ibid.). Applied to a prototype at TRL 7, the actual system at 8.           |
| **Simulated operational environment** | A real environment reproducing all operational requirements, or a simulated one allowing a virtual prototype to be tested (ibid.).                                                                                   |
| **Breadboard**                        | "Integrated components that provide a representation of a system/subsystem… typically configured for laboratory use… may resemble final system/subsystem in function only" (ibid.).                                  |
| **Brassboard**                        | Common usage, not in the sources held here: beyond a breadboard — closer to final form, fit and function, built for testing outside the laboratory but not production-representative.                                |
| **Prototype**                         | "A physical or virtual model used to evaluate the technical or manufacturing feasibility or military utility of a particular technology or process, concept, end item, or system" (ibid.).                           |
| **Alpha / beta prototype**            | EERE usage: alpha is the stand-alone component validated in the laboratory (TRL 4); beta is the component (5) or system (6) validated in a relevant environment (`eere-r540-112-02`, p. 1).                          |
| **High / low fidelity**               | High fidelity "addresses form, fit, and function"; a low-fidelity representation gives little beyond first-order information (`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3, p. 11).                                                     |
| **Model**                             | "A functional form of a system, generally reduced in scale, near or at operational specification" (ibid.).                                                                                                           |
| **Qualification**                     | The test and demonstration campaign showing the actual system meets its specifications across the expected range — the substance of TRL 8.                                                                           |

## Other

| Term      | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **FMECA** | Failure Modes, Effects and Criticality Analysis. Required from TRL 4 upwards by the marine framework's tailoring.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **CUI**   | Controlled Unclassified Information: a U.S. Government designation for information needing safeguarding but not classified. Never enter CUI, classified or export-controlled material into a tool hosted publicly — use the "Sensitive — reference only" marking to point at it instead. |
