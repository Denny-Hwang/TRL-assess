# Glossary

**TRL — Technology Readiness Level.** A 1–9 scale describing how far a technology has been
demonstrated, and in what environment. Not a measure of quality, value or programme health.

**TRA — Technology Readiness Assessment.** A formal, independent evaluation of the maturity of a
programme's Critical Technology Elements, conducted under an agency process. This tool produces a
_self-assessment_, which is not a TRA.

**CTE — Critical Technology Element.** A part of the system whose maturity decides whether the
system works: new, or newly used, with real uncertainty about whether it will perform. TRAs assess
CTEs, not whole products.

**Relevant environment.** "Testing environment that simulates both the most important and most
stressing aspects of the operational environment" (`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3, p. 11). Applied to a
component at TRL 5 and to a system or subsystem at TRL 6.

**Operational environment.** "Environment that addresses user operational requirements and
specifications required of the final system to include platform/packaging" (`dod-tra-2025`,
Table 2-3, p. 11). Applied to a prototype at TRL 7 and to the actual system at TRL 8.

**Simulated operational environment.** Either a real environment that can reproduce all the
operational requirements, or a simulated one allowing a virtual prototype to be tested
(`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3, p. 11).

**Breadboard.** "Integrated components that provide a representation of a system/subsystem and that
can be used to determine concept feasibility and to develop technical data. Typically configured for
laboratory use… May resemble final system/subsystem in function only" (`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3,
p. 11).

**Brassboard.** Common usage (not defined in the sources this tool holds): a step beyond a
breadboard — closer to final form, fit and function, built for testing outside the laboratory but
not production-representative.

**Prototype.** "A physical or virtual model used to evaluate the technical or manufacturing
feasibility or military utility of a particular technology or process, concept, end item, or system"
(`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3, p. 11).

**Alpha / beta prototype.** EERE usage: an alpha prototype is the stand-alone component validated in
the laboratory (TRL 4); a beta prototype is the component (TRL 5) or system (TRL 6) validated in a
relevant environment (`eere-r540-112-02`, p. 1).

**High fidelity / low fidelity.** High fidelity "addresses form, fit, and function"; a low-fidelity
representation gives little beyond first-order information (`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3, p. 11).

**Model.** "A functional form of a system, generally reduced in scale, near or at operational
specification" (`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-3, p. 11).

**Qualification.** The test and demonstration campaign that shows the actual system meets its
specifications across the expected range of conditions — the substance of TRL 8.

**TPL — Technology Performance Level.** NREL's scale for _how well a technology would perform if
mature_ (cost, survivability, environmental compliance). Orthogonal to TRL.

**MRL — Manufacturing Readiness Level.** DoD's 1–10 scale for manufacturing process, supply chain
and quality-system maturity. A high TRL with a low MRL means demonstrable but not producible.

**IRL — Integration Readiness Level.** Maturity of the interface between two elements.

**SRL — System Readiness Level.** A composite view combining TRLs and IRLs across a system.

**ARL — Adoption Readiness Level.** DOE Office of Technology Transitions' framework for the
non-technical barriers between a working technology and its use.

**GOOS Framework for Ocean Observing.** Classifies observing elements as _concept_, _pilot_ or
_mature_ across requirements, observations and data management — the instrument and its data
pipeline together.

**FMECA.** Failure Modes, Effects and Criticality Analysis. Required from TRL 4 upwards by the
marine framework's tailoring.

**Evidence coverage.** In this tool: criteria marked _Met_ that have at least one non-rejected
evidence item, divided by criteria marked _Met_.

**Contiguity.** The rule that a level counts only if every level below it is achieved. Applied in
both tiers.

**CUI — Controlled Unclassified Information.** A U.S. Government designation for information that
requires safeguarding but is not classified. Generic note: do not enter CUI, classified or
export-controlled material into a tool hosted on a public site. Use the "Sensitive — reference only"
marking to point at such material instead.
