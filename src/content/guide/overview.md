# What a Technology Readiness Level is

A TRL is a number from 1 to 9 that answers one question: **how far has this technology been
demonstrated, and in what environment?** It does not measure quality, market value or how well the
project is run.

:::figure trl-scale:::

## The nine levels

The definitions below are the DoD hardware definitions (`dod-tra-2025`, Table 2-1, pp. 6–7). Each
framework shows its own definition in the help text of every question.

| TRL | Definition                                                                         | Environment            |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1   | Basic principles observed and reported                                             | —                      |
| 2   | Technology concept and/or application formulated                                   | analysis               |
| 3   | Analytical and experimental critical function and/or characteristic proof of concept | laboratory             |
| 4   | Component and/or breadboard validation in a laboratory environment                 | laboratory             |
| 5   | Component and/or breadboard validation in a relevant environment                   | relevant               |
| 6   | System/subsystem model or prototype demonstration in a relevant environment        | relevant               |
| 7   | System prototype demonstration in an operational environment                       | operational            |
| 8   | Actual system completed and qualified through test and demonstration               | expected conditions    |
| 9   | Actual system proven through successful mission operations                         | mission conditions     |

The level is decided by _what_ was tested and _where_.

## A TRL needs its framework

Agencies broadly share the nine definitions but not the criteria for claiming a level. "TRL 6" under
one framework is not the same claim as "TRL 6" under another, so every export records the framework
id and version. See [Frameworks & sources](/guide/frameworks).

## Where this tool fits

A self-assessment aid with two tiers: a **quick estimate** in a few minutes with no documents, and an
**evidence-based assessment** where every claim is tied to a document, a test record, a pinned commit
or a DOI.

It is **not** an independent Technology Readiness Assessment (TRA). A TRA is run by a team
independent of the programme and can reject your evidence. This tool records what you enter and
applies the rules conservatively. Use it to prepare for a TRA or to find missing evidence early.
